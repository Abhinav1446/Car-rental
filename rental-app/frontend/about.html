About us

A small fleet, run properly.

We're a locally owned self-drive rental service — every car in our fleet is maintained and booked directly by us, not through a third-party marketplace.

How we operate

We keep things simple on purpose. There's no call center and no middleman — when you book a car, you're dealing directly with the person who owns and maintains it. That means faster answers, fewer surprises, and someone who actually knows the vehicle you're renting.

What to expect

  • Clear, upfront per-day pricing — no hidden fees added at pickup.
  • Straightforward UPI payment, confirmed personally for every booking.
  • A small fleet, which means vehicles are looked after between every rental.
  • Direct WhatsApp contact if your plans change or you have questions.

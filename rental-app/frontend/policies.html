Privacy

What we collect

When you make a booking or send an enquiry, we collect your name, phone number, and the rental dates/vehicle you're interested in. If you message us on WhatsApp, we also see whatever you choose to share there.

How it's used

This information is used only to process and confirm your booking, contact you about it, and handle payment verification. We don't sell your information to third parties, and don't use it for marketing without your separate consent.

Where it's stored

Booking information is stored securely with our hosting and database providers. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but as with any online service, we can't guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

You can ask us to review, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us (see Contact).
